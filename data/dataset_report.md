## 4. Label normalisation

Rule (implemented in `lingestra/labels.py`):

1. Clean whitespace and capitalise the first letter (`brother` -> `Brother`).
2. Group by (category, case-insensitive label). Every clip in a group is a *take* of one sign. This covers `-01/-02` variants and duplicate pages with an identical slug (many Numbers appear twice).
3. A trailing ` N` is **not** stripped automatically: real numbers (`11`, `100`) and genuinely numbered signs (`National Center For Statistics 1/2`, `Qasrul Emiraat 1/2`) must stay, and wrongly merging two different signs into one class hurts more than keeping two near-duplicate classes. Only entries whose landmarks are near-identical to the base sign (mean abs. difference < 0.1) are merged via `labels.MERGE_TO`.

Evidence: mean absolute difference between trimmed+resampled normalised clips (lower = more similar). Random sign pairs: 5th pct 0.18, 25th 0.31, median 0.38. Clips known to be the same label (n=91): median 0.25, 25th 0.14, 75th 0.37.

**Uncertainty:** the same-label and random distributions overlap strongly (the metric has no time alignment and signers/framing differ), so landmark similarity is weak evidence. Only very close matches are merged; everything else stays separate and should be reviewed by a native signer. Suffix entries found:

## 6. Limitations (be honest in the demo)

- About one take per sign; the 'real held-out take' test exists for only a minority of signs.
- One signer (probably), studio conditions: plain background, good lighting, frontal camera, no webcam noise. Accuracy on the training dictionary measures robustness to speed/position/angle augmentation, **not** generalisation to a new signer. Expect lower accuracy on a real user's webcam.
- Landmark quality varies: some two-handed signs lose a hand in many frames.
- Labels are the site's English slugs and contain typos (e.g. `Naphew`) and category mix-ups (e.g. `Absher Card` listed under Health). Not corrected, never translated.
- Demo phrases are word sequences for scripting, not grammatical sentences.
