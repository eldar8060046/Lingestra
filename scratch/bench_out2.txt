# Recognition benchmark

Generated by `python scripts/benchmark_recognition.py` (seed 0). Do not edit by hand.

## Honest scope

* All data are the UAE Sign Language Dictionary landmarks: **one dictionary signer** (plus a few re-takes). The cross-take test (template = take 1, query = take 2 of the same sign) is the most realistic proxy available; it is NOT a test on a new signer or on your webcam.
* **Real webcam validation by the team is still required** (different camera, lighting, framing, the signer's own style). Numbers below will differ in daily use. Enrolling 2-3 of your own takes per sign is recommended, but this benchmark only verifies that extra takes do NOT loosen rejection (open-set 1-take vs 2-take table); it cannot measure the gain from the user's own takes.
* Pool: 1102 signs with a good-quality clip, 101 with >= 2 good takes. Vocabulary of size V = V random eligible signs, one enrolled template each (take 1), queries = take 2. Sizes with repeats: [10, 30, 100] x 5 random draws (mean).
* Columns: **top-1** = nearest enrolled sign is the right one (ignores rejection); **accepted ok** = answered 'sign' with the right label; **wrong** = answered 'sign' with a WRONG label; **clarify ok** = asked 'did you mean A or B' and the right sign is one of the two; **unknown** = rejected. accepted ok + wrong + clarify (ok or not) + unknown = 100%. **precision** = accepted ok / (accepted ok + wrong): of the words actually written, the share that were right. **absorbing labels** = the labels that received the most wrong answers (summed over draws): a label that dominates is an 'attractor'.
* **How to read the cross-take stress rows:** they barely move (0-5 points) because the difference between two dictionary takes of the SAME sign dominates; they are a floor set by take mismatch, NOT evidence of speed / pause invariance. The evidence for invariance is the same-take table, where that difference is removed.

## Speed

50 signs / 150 templates (takes): enrolment (feature extraction + calibration) 1.50 s, then **8.0 ms mean / 8.8 ms max per decision** (made once per signed sign, not per frame; budget was < 30 ms).
